# What you sell, and how your emails sound

Status: EXAMPLE. Your agent asks you and replaces this line with "Status: yours" once the file is yours.

## The offer

- **Product:** Evaboot exports a LinkedIn Sales Navigator search to a clean spreadsheet, with verified professional emails.
- **For:** sales leaders who build or rebuild their team's prospect lists.
- **Why it matters to them:** clean lists with emails in minutes instead of manual copy-paste, and fewer bounces.
- **Proof you may use:** none beyond the product facts above. Never invent customers, numbers or results.
- **Ask:** a low-effort question, for example "Worth a look?" or "Open to a 2-minute demo video?". Never a 30-minute meeting in the first email.

## Rules for every email

1. **Subject: 60 characters at most**, lower case, about them, not about you.
2. **Body: 40 to 110 words.** Open with "Hi <first name>,". One short paragraph on them, one on the offer, one line for the ask.
3. **One real hook per email**, named: the new role, the company's growth, the size of the sales team, or one line of the lead's own profile. Nothing from memory or the web.
4. **No link, no attachment, no image** in a first email: they hurt deliverability.
5. **No template phrases** ("hope this finds you", "quick question", "game-changer"...). The check script lists them.
6. **Vary the first line.** No opening on more than a third of the emails.
7. **Skip** a lead with no verified email, or with nothing real to say, and write the reason.
